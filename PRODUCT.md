# Jeremy Moore Tech Blog

## Brand Identity

**Who:** Developers (beginner to advanced) seeking practical tips on web development and Linux
**What:** A personal tech blog sharing actionable tips, tutorials, and how-tos
**Why:** Personal brand building, lead generation, establishing expertise
**Tone:** Technical & authoritative, yet approachable and welcoming. Expert without gatekeeping.

## Strategic Principles

- **Authority through clarity:** Clean, minimal design lets the content shine. No decoration obscures credibility.
- **Professional polish:** Every detail matters. Sticky header, readable typography, proper spacing signal "I know what I'm doing."
- **Conversion-focused:** Newsletter signup and CTAs are visible everywhere, but never aggressive.
- **Dark + focused:** Dark theme suits developer audiences; orange accents provide energy without overwhelming.

## Register

**Brand** - The design IS the personal brand. Credibility, polish, and professionalism are the product.

## Users

- **Primary:** Web developers (early career to senior) looking for specific tips and how-tos
- **Secondary:** DevOps engineers, Linux enthusiasts
- **Tertiary:** Potential clients/collaborators evaluating expertise

## Anti-References

- Avoid: Generic SaaS templates, flashy animations, overstimulation
- Avoid: Crypto/web3 aesthetics, glassmorphism, gradient text
- Avoid: Inconsistent spacing, blurry or unreadable headers
- Avoid: Generic hero metrics or clichéd layouts

## Accessibility

- Large base font (18px) for readability
- High contrast: light text (#e0e0e0) on dark (#0f0f0f)
- Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation
- Proper heading hierarchy
